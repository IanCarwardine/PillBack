# PillBack User Guide

Welcome to PillBack, your medication timing tracker designed to help you maintain consistent medication schedules.

## Getting Started

### First Launch

When you first open PillBack, you'll be guided through a simple setup:

1. **Welcome** - Learn what PillBack does
2. **Your Name** - Enter your name (or skip to use default)
3. **Notifications** - Enable reminders (recommended)
4. **Schedule Setup** - Set your wake and sleep times

### Your Daily Schedule

PillBack creates a 6-dose schedule based on your waking hours. Each dose corresponds to a **Port** (1-6) on your pill organizer.

## Main Screen

### Schedule Tab

This is your main view showing today's doses.

**Dose Cards** show:
- Port number (1-6)
- Scheduled time
- Medications in that port
- Status (Pending, Taken, Missed)

**To mark a dose as taken:**
1. Find the dose card
2. Tap the green **Take** button
3. The dose will be marked with your timing accuracy

**To edit a taken dose:**
1. Tap the dose card to expand it
2. Tap **Edit** at the bottom
3. Adjust the time if needed
4. Tap **Update Time** to save

### Timeline Sidebar

The left sidebar shows a quick view of all doses:
- Gray dot = Pending
- Green dot = Taken on time
- Yellow dot = Taken late
- Checkmark = Completed

**Expand/Collapse:** Tap the arrow button at the top of the sidebar.

### Medications Tab

View and manage your medications:
- **KEY DRUG** - Your primary medication (usually Levodopa)
- **Other Medications** - All other pills

**To add a medication:**
1. Tap the **+** button
2. Enter medication details
3. Select which ports contain this medication
4. Tap **Save**

### Adherence Tab

Track your timing accuracy:

**Overall Score** - Your daily timing accuracy percentage

**Dose Breakdown** - See how each dose scored:
- Perfect (green) - Within 5 minutes
- Good (dark green) - Within 10 minutes
- Fair (amber) - Within 20 minutes
- Review (coral) - More than 20 minutes off

**Pattern Alerts** - PillBack detects if certain ports are consistently late and suggests schedule adjustments.

### Settings Tab

Customize your experience:

**Personal**
- Your display name

**Schedule**
- Wake time - When your day starts
- Sleep time - When your day ends
- Strategy - How doses are spaced
  - *Equal Distribution* - Evenly spread across the day
  - *Fixed Interval* - Based on KEY DRUG timing

**Appearance**
- Choose from 4 themes:
  - Clinical (dark green)
  - Warm (brown/amber)
  - High Contrast (accessibility)
  - Daylight (light mode)

**Data**
- Export (coming soon)
- Reset all data

## Timing Categories Explained

| Category | Timing | What It Means |
|----------|--------|---------------|
| **Perfect** | Within 5 min | Excellent timing |
| **Good** | Within 10 min | Minor variation, acceptable |
| **Fair** | Within 20 min | Some impact on medication levels |
| **Review** | Over 20 min | Consider adjusting your routine |

## Tips for Better Timing

1. **Set realistic times** - Choose wake/sleep times that match your actual routine
2. **Enable notifications** - Get reminders before each dose
3. **Keep organizer visible** - Place your pill organizer where you'll see it
4. **Check patterns** - Review the Adherence tab to spot problem times
5. **Adjust if needed** - Use Settings to change your schedule

## Timeline Only Mode

For a simpler view:
1. Tap **Timeline Only** (top right)
2. See all doses in a focused list
3. Tap any dose to mark it as taken
4. Tap **Full View** to return

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What if I forget to mark a dose?**
A: You can still mark it later. Expand the dose card and tap Take, then edit the time to when you actually took it.

**Q: Can I undo marking a dose?**
A: Yes! Tap the dose card, tap Edit, then tap "Mark as Not Taken".

**Q: Why does my schedule reset every day?**
A: PillBack creates a fresh schedule each day at midnight so you always have accurate times.

**Q: What's a KEY DRUG?**
A: Your primary medication that requires consistent timing (usually Levodopa for Parkinson's patients). It's marked with a star.

**Q: How do I change my dose times?**
A: Go to Settings → Schedule and adjust your wake/sleep times. Tap "Regenerate Schedule" to apply.

## Troubleshooting

**Notifications not working?**
- Go to iPhone Settings → PillBack → Notifications
- Make sure notifications are enabled

**Schedule times look wrong?**
- Check your wake/sleep times in Settings
- Try "Regenerate Schedule"

**Need to start over?**
- Settings → Reset All Data (warning: this deletes everything)
